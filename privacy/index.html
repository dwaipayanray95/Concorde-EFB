Privacy Policy

Concorde EFB — Windows, macOS and Android app, and this website.
Effective date: 3 October 2026 (updated for Wi-Fi sharing)

Concorde EFB is a free, independent flight-planning tool for the DC Designs Concorde in Microsoft Flight Simulator. It is for flight simulation only and is not affiliated with DC Designs, Microsoft, British Airways or Air France.

In short: the app has no accounts and we do not collect, store or sell your personal data. Your settings stay on your device. The Android version shows ads from Google AdMob, which processes some device data as described below.

1. Data stored on your device

The app saves your preferences (light/dark theme, your SimBrief username) and a cached copy of the public airport database on your own device. Nothing is uploaded to us. Uninstalling the app removes it.

2. Flight simulator data

On Windows, the Flight Monitor reads live data from Microsoft Flight Simulator through a small helper program that runs on your computer. By default this data never leaves your computer.

If you turn on Share to phone / tablet, the helper also makes the data available on your own local network (protected by a 6-digit pairing code) and announces itself there so your phone or tablet can find it. The data goes directly between your devices; it is never sent to us or to the internet.

4. Advertising (Android only)

The Android app shows banner ads from Google AdMob. The desktop apps and this website show no ads. To show and measure ads, Google may collect and use your device's advertising ID, IP address, device and app information, and ad interactions. Learn more in How Google uses information from apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

Your choices:

  • If you are in the European Economic Area, the UK or Switzerland, the app asks for your consent (using Google's certified consent tool) before ads use your data for personalisation. You can change your choice at any time with AD PRIVACY OPTIONS in the app's footer.
  • On any Android device you can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalised ads, in Settings → Google → Ads (or Settings → Privacy → Ads).

5. This website

This site is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may log visitor IP addresses for security (see the GitHub Privacy Statement). Pages load fonts from Google Fonts. The site uses no analytics and no ads.

6. Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect data from children.

7. Selling or sharing data

We do not sell, rent or share personal data. We have no servers that store user data.

8. Your rights

Because we hold no personal data about you, there is nothing for us to access, correct or delete. For data processed by Google AdMob or the services listed above, use their own privacy controls.

9. Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date. Significant changes will also be noted in the changelog.

10. Contact
